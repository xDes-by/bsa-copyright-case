Problem description

My custom game was copied without permission: code, artwork, UI visual style, gameplay, balance, quests, events, localization, and more.

04 — breach of the sworn statement

The counter-notice and its perjury clause

I have the correspondence, including the counter-notice he filed under Steam's procedure. One of the mandatory clauses in such a document requires the filer to state, under penalty of perjury, that they have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.

The screenshots and materials collected on this page (decompiled files containing the original BSA map and name, matching item and ability artwork, copied code with my nickname in it, localization copied wholesale without even changing the title, and correspondence with the person hired to do the copying) together show that the takedown was not a mistake or a misidentification. In my view, that means the statement made under oath in the counter-notice was not true.
